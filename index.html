FAQ

Water Damage Restoration Questions, Answered

What's the difference between water mitigation and restoration? +

Mitigation stops further damage by extracting water and drying the structure. Restoration is the repair phase after: replacing drywall, flooring, and rebuilding. Most companies, including ours, handle both in one job.

How much does water damage restoration cost in Kenosha? +

Most residential jobs range from $1,000 to $8,000+ depending on water category, square footage affected, and whether structural materials need replacing. We provide a written estimate before work starts.

Does homeowners insurance cover water damage? +

Sudden events like a burst pipe are typically covered; gradual leaks and sewer backup often require a separate rider. We help document damage for your claim either way.

How fast does mold start growing after water damage? +

Mold can begin colonizing within 24-48 hours of water exposure, which is why fast extraction and drying matters more than almost anything else in the process.

How do I know a restoration company isn't scamming me? +

Get a written scope of work before anything starts, be wary of anyone offering to waive your deductible (that's insurance fraud), and don't feel pressured to sign an assignment of benefits on the spot.

Should I call my insurance company or a restoration company first? +

Call a restoration company first if water is actively spreading: every hour raises the risk of mold and structural damage. We can begin mitigation immediately and help you file the claim afterward; most policies allow this.

What is Category 3 (black) water and why does it cost more? +

Category 3 water is grossly contaminated: sewage backups and floodwater fall into this class. It requires biohazard-level PPE, disposal of porous materials that can't be sanitized, and stricter containment, which increases labor and material costs versus clean-water (Category 1) jobs.

Can my furniture and belongings be saved after water damage? +

Often yes, if it's clean water and addressed quickly. We offer content pack-out, cleaning, and storage during the drying process for items that can be restored, and document anything that can't for your insurance claim.

Do I need a second quote before starting insurance-covered work? +

Not usually required, but it's reasonable to get one if you're unsure about scope or pricing, especially for larger jobs. We provide a written, itemized estimate so you have something concrete to compare.
