What Should You Do If a Pipe Freezes or Bursts?

If a pipe freezes or bursts in your Kenosha home, shut off your main water valve immediately, avoid contact with outlets or standing water near electrical hazards, and document the damage with photos before moving anything. Begin emergency water removal right away and call a restoration professional quickly, since burst pipes often cause the most damage when temperatures rise and frozen pipes begin thawing, releasing pressurized water into walls, floors, and ceilings.

  1. Shut off your main water supply
  2. Avoid electrical hazards near standing water
  3. Document the damage with photos
  4. Begin emergency water removal
  5. Call a water damage restoration company
Local Winter Risk

Why Frozen Pipes Are Common in Kenosha Homes

Kenosha's Wisconsin winters bring long stretches of subfreezing temperatures off Lake Michigan, and much of the city's housing stock, built well before modern insulation standards, wasn't designed for today's energy codes. Drafty basements, uninsulated crawl spaces, and supply lines run through exterior walls all leave pipes exposed to cold air for hours at a time. Once water inside a pipe freezes, pressure builds behind the ice blockage, and the pipe can crack or split within hours; the actual flooding often doesn't show up until that frozen pipe begins to thaw and pressurized water starts pouring out behind drywall.

What Causes Frozen & Burst Pipes in Kenosha

Pipes located near exterior walls and in unheated areas of the home face the highest risk of freezing and bursting during a Wisconsin cold snap.

Crawl Spaces

🚪

Garage Plumbing

Detached and attached garages rarely stay warm enough to protect exposed supply lines.

⚡

A lost furnace or heat source during a storm lets indoor temperatures drop fast enough to freeze pipes.

Mold Prevention

Prevent Mold Growth After Pipe Damage

Mold can begin colonizing wet materials within 24-48 hours of water exposure, which is why fast extraction and drying after a burst pipe matters more than almost any other step in the process. Wet drywall, waterlogged insulation, and hidden moisture trapped inside wall cavities are the most common places mold takes hold after pipe damage, often before it's ever visible from the room itself.

Prevention

How to Prevent Frozen Pipes in Wisconsin

A few simple habits go a long way toward avoiding a frozen or burst pipe during a Kenosha cold snap.

  • Insulate exposed pipes in basements, attics, and crawl spaces
  • Apply heat tape to vulnerable supply lines
  • Keep basements and crawl spaces heated through winter
  • Open sink cabinets on exterior walls during severe cold
  • Let faucets drip slightly during extreme cold snaps
  • Know the location of your main water shutoff valve

FAQ

Frozen & Burst Pipe Repair Questions, Answered

What causes pipes to burst in winter?Toggle answer

Water expands as it freezes, and the pressure that builds up behind an ice blockage inside a pipe can crack or split the pipe wall. This most often happens in poorly insulated areas like exterior walls, crawl spaces, unheated basements, and garages, where the pipe is exposed to prolonged subfreezing temperatures.

How quickly can a burst pipe flood a home?

A burst pipe can release hundreds of gallons of water in a matter of hours, especially if it goes unnoticed overnight or while a home is unoccupied. Water spreads quickly behind walls, under flooring, and into ceilings below, which is why shutting off the main water supply immediately is critical.

Does homeowners insurance cover burst pipe damage?

Most homeowners policies cover burst pipe damage because it's considered sudden and accidental, unlike a slow, gradual leak that may be excluded. Coverage details vary by policy, so we help document the damage thoroughly to support your claim either way. See our guide to insurance coverage for water damage for more detail.

How long does burst pipe water damage restoration take?

Most residential burst pipe jobs take 3-5 days to fully dry using industrial air movers and dehumidifiers before repairs begin. Larger jobs, or ones involving mold growth or structural repairs, can take longer. See our full breakdown at how long water damage restoration takes.

Can frozen pipes thaw without bursting?

Yes, a frozen pipe can thaw safely without bursting if the ice blockage clears before pressure builds enough to crack the pipe wall. However, many frozen pipes have already cracked while frozen and simply haven't leaked yet, so damage frequently appears only after thawing begins.

What are signs of hidden water damage after a pipe leak?

Bubbling or peeling paint, soft or discolored drywall, musty odors, and unexplained increases in your water bill can all indicate hidden water damage behind walls or under flooring. Our guide to signs of hidden water damage covers the full list.

How much does burst pipe cleanup cost in Kenosha?

Burst pipe cleanup costs depend on the water category, how far the water traveled, and whether structural materials need replacing, but most Kenosha jobs range from $1,000 to $8,000 or more. We provide a written scope of work before any work begins. See real ranges at cost of water damage restoration in Kenosha.

How can I prevent frozen pipes in Wisconsin?

Insulating exposed pipes, keeping basements and crawl spaces heated, opening cabinet doors on exterior-wall plumbing, and letting faucets drip during severe cold are all commonly recommended Wisconsin winter prevention measures. Knowing your main shutoff location also limits damage if a pipe does burst.
