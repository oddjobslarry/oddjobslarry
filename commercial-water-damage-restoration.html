What Should a Business Do After Water Damage?

Protect employees and customers first, then stop the water source if it's safe to do so. Document the damage with photos, protect inventory and equipment, and begin emergency water extraction right away. Contact a commercial restoration company immediately, since a fast response reduces business interruption, inventory loss, and mold growth risk. Commercial restoration focuses on both property recovery and operational continuity, not just drying the building.

Commercial Drying

Commercial Structural Drying Services

Commercial drying relies on extraction, airflow, dehumidification, and ongoing moisture monitoring, scaled to the size of the facility, whether it's a warehouse, office building, retail space, multi-family property, or school.

Equipment and methods: LGR dehumidifiers, desiccant drying for large or humid spaces, psychrometric calculations to set drying targets, industrial air movers, and continuous moisture monitoring with drying logs documented for insurance purposes.

Mold Prevention

Prevent Mold Growth in Commercial Buildings

Hidden moisture in wet insulation, ceiling cavities, and wall systems is the most common source of commercial mold problems, and it also affects indoor air quality for employees and customers.

Mold can begin developing within 24–48 hours if materials remain wet. Fast extraction and structural drying are the most effective way to prevent it. Learn more in our guide to commercial mold remediation after water damage.
