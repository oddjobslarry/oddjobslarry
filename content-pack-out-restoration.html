What Is a Content Pack-Out Service?

A content pack-out is the process of inventorying, photographing, packing, labeling, and transporting the belongings inside a home or business so structural drying and repairs can proceed safely. Items are cleaned or restored where possible, held in secure storage, and returned once repairs are complete. Pack-outs are commonly used after water damage, flooding, mold remediation, sewage contamination, and major reconstruction projects, and they typically run alongside your insurance contents claim.

FAQ

Content Pack-Out & Restoration Questions, Answered

What is a content pack-out?

A content pack-out is the process of inventorying, photographing, packing, and transporting belongings so structural drying and repairs can proceed safely. Items are cleaned or restored where possible, stored securely, and returned once the space is ready.

Can water-damaged furniture be restored?

Often yes. Furniture exposed to clean or grey water and addressed quickly can typically be cleaned, dried, and restored. Furniture exposed to Category 3 (black) water or prolonged moisture may not be salvageable and will be documented for your insurance claim instead.

How are belongings tracked during restoration?

Every item is photographed, logged in an itemized inventory, and labeled with a tracking number before it leaves your property. This chain-of-custody documentation follows the item through cleaning, storage, and pack-back.

Does insurance cover contents restoration?

Most homeowners policies include contents coverage alongside dwelling coverage, though limits and exclusions vary. We provide the photo inventory and condition reports adjusters typically require to process a contents claim.

How long are items stored during restoration?

Storage duration matches the structural drying and repair timeline, typically a few days for straightforward drying jobs and several weeks for larger reconstruction projects.

Can documents and photos be saved after water damage?

Many documents and photographs can be recovered if they are freeze-dried or specially cleaned soon after exposure. The longer they sit wet, the lower the odds of full recovery.

What happens to items that cannot be restored?

Non-restorable items are photographed, itemized, and documented in a condition report before disposal so the loss is properly reflected in your insurance claim.

How much does content pack-out restoration cost in Kenosha?

Cost depends on the volume of contents, water category, and whether items need specialty cleaning or extended storage. We provide a written estimate after a free contents assessment before any work begins.
